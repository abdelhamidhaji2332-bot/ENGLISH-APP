Histoire & Geographie — BAC Methodology (منهجية التاريخ والجغرافيا)

Introduction (مقدمة)

Welcome to the H&G BAC methodology guide. This guide covers everything you need to know for the BAC exam in History and Geography.

The BAC H&G exam consists of two main parts:

  • Part 1: Document Analysis — analyze one or more documents (texts, graphs, maps) and write a structured commentary
  • Part 2: Essay — write a structured essay on a historical or geographical topic

1. Document Analysis Methodology (منهجية تحليل الوثائق)

Step 1: Presentation (العرض)

Identify the document by answering these 5 questions:

  1. Nature: What type of document? (text, map, graph, cartoon, table, photo)
  2. Author: Who created it? (if known — name, position, background)
  3. Source: Where does it come from? (book title, newspaper, archive, website)
  4. Date: When was it created? (exact date or period)
  5. Main Idea: What is the document about in one sentence? (the central theme)
Example: "The document is a text entitled 'The Cold War: A New World Order' written by historian John Smith in 2005. It discusses the geopolitical tensions between the USA and the USSR after World War II."

Step 2: Analysis (التحليل)

  • Extract key information from the document
  • Identify the main ideas and secondary ideas
  • Classify information into categories: political, economic, social, cultural
  • Quote or paraphrase relevant passages
  • Explain the meaning in your own words
  • Highlight important dates, figures, names
Useful phrases:
"L'auteur affirme que..." (The author states that...)
"Le document met en evidence..." (The document highlights...)
"On peut distinguer deux idees principales..." (We can distinguish two main ideas...)

Step 3: Commentary / Critique (التعليق والنقد)

  • Evaluate the document's reliability and perspective
  • What is the author's bias or point of view?
  • Is the document complete? What is missing?
  • How does it compare to other sources on the same topic?
  • What is the document's value for understanding the topic?
Useful phrases:
"Ce document est objectif car..." (This document is objective because...)
"L'auteur adopte un point de vue..." (The author adopts a viewpoint of...)
"Cependant, il faut nuancer cette affirmation car..." (However, this statement must be qualified because...)

2. Essay Construction (بناء المقال)

Introduction (المقدمة)

The introduction should contain:

  1. General opening: Start with a broad statement about the topic
  2. Define key terms: Explain important concepts or dates
  3. Problem/question: State what the essay will address
  4. Announce the plan: Briefly outline the structure (e.g., "First, we will analyze... Second, we will examine... Finally, we will conclude...")

Body (2-3 parts) (العرض)

Part 1: Present the first aspect of the topic

Part 2: Analyze causes, consequences, or developments

Part 3 (optional): Discuss different perspectives or counter-arguments

Each paragraph should follow: Idea → Explanation → Example → Mini-conclusion

Use transition phrases between paragraphs.

Conclusion (الخاتمة)

  1. Summarize the main findings from each part
  2. Answer the question posed in the introduction
  3. Provide a personal, well-reasoned judgment (if required)
  4. Optional: Open a perspective for future developments

3. Graph Commentary (تحليل المبيان)

Types of Graphs

  • Line graph (courbe): Shows evolution over time
  • Bar chart (histogramme): Compares quantities
  • Pie chart (diagramme circulaire): Shows proportions

Analysis Steps

  1. Presentation: Nature, source, title, variables (axes), date
  2. Description: Overall trend (increase, decrease, stability), significant points, peaks, troughs
  3. Interpretation: Explain the causes and consequences of the observed trends
  4. Critique: Limitations of the data, missing information, reliability
Useful phrases:
"Le graphique montre une evolution croissante de..." (The graph shows an increasing trend of...)
"On observe une stagnation entre..." (We observe a stagnation between...)
"Cette augmentation s'explique par..." (This increase is explained by...)

4. Map Work (الخريطة)

  • Title: What does the map represent?
  • Legend: Understand all symbols and colors
  • Spatial distribution: Where are things located?
  • Scale: Understand distances and proportions
  • Interpretation: Explain the spatial patterns observed

5. Timeline Work (الخط الزمني)

  • Place events in chronological order
  • Identify cause-and-effect relationships between events
  • Recognize periods and turning points
  • Connect events to broader historical contexts

8. Useful Phrases by Section (عبارات مفيدة)

For Introduction

"Dans le cadre de l'etude de..." (In the context of studying...)
"Ce document s'inscrit dans..." (This document fits into...)
"Nous allons analyser..." (We are going to analyze...)
"Notre reflexion s'organisera en..." (Our reflection will be organized in...)

For Analysis/Body

"Tout d'abord, il convient de..." (First of all, it is appropriate to...)
"En premier lieu, on constate que..." (Firstly, we note that...)
"Par ailleurs, il faut souligner..." (Furthermore, we must emphasize...)
"En revanche, on peut observer..." (On the other hand, we can observe...)
"Ainsi, on peut dire que..." (Thus, we can say that...)

For Conclusion

"En conclusion, on peut affirmer que..." (In conclusion, we can affirm that...)
"Pour conclure, ce document montre que..." (To conclude, this document shows that...)
"En definitive, il est clair que..." (Ultimately, it is clear that...)

9. Tips & Advice (نصائح)

✓ Read the subject carefully — underline key words.

✓ Plan your answer before starting to write (5 minutes).

✓ Use the document's information but don't just copy it — analyze it.

✓ Structure your answer clearly with paragraphs.

✓ Use specific dates, names, and figures to support your arguments.

✓ Define key terms when you first use them.

✓ Use transition phrases between paragraphs.

✓ Write legibly — your examiner must be able to read your answer.

✓ Manage your time: 1 hour per part.

✓ Review your work in the last 5 minutes.
