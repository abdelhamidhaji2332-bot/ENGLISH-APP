Reported Speech (Indirect Speech) (الكلام المنقول)

Direct vs. Reported

Direct Speech: The exact words someone said, in quotation marks.

Reported Speech: Reporting what someone said without using their exact words.

Direct: "I am happy," she said.
Reported: She said that she was happy.

Tense Changes (Backshift)

When the reporting verb is in the past tense (said, told, asked), the tense moves "one step back":

Direct SpeechReported Speech
Present Simple: "I like coffee."Past Simple: He said he liked coffee.
Present Continuous: "I am reading."Past Continuous: He said he was reading.
Past Simple: "I saw her."Past Perfect: He said he had seen her.
Present Perfect: "I have finished."Past Perfect: He said he had finished.
will: "I will come."would: He said he would come.
can: "I can swim."could: He said he could swim.
must: "I must go."had to: He said he had to go.
may: "I may be late."might: He said he might be late.

Pronoun Changes

"I love my country." → He said he loved his country.
"You should see your doctor." → She told me I should see my doctor.

Questions in Reported Speech

Yes/No questions: Use "if" or "whether"

"Do you like coffee?" → He asked if I liked coffee.
"Are you coming?" → She asked whether I was coming.

Wh- questions: Keep the question word, use statement word order

"Where do you live?" → She asked where I lived.
"Why are you late?" → He asked why I was late.

Commands & Requests

Form: told/asked + to + infinitive

"Sit down!" → He told me to sit down.
"Don't smoke!" → She told me not to smoke.
"Please help me." → She asked me to help her.

BAC Exam Tips

  • Remember to change both the tense AND the pronouns.
  • Don't forget to change time/place references.
  • The word "that" is optional in reported speech.
  • If the reporting verb is in present tense (says), no backshift is needed.
