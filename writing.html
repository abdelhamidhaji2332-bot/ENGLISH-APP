BAC Writing Guide (دليل الكتابة للباك)

Types of BAC Writing Tasks

  • Formal Letter — apply, complain, request information
  • Informal Letter — write to friends/family
  • Article — for school magazine or newspaper
  • Essay — opinion, argumentative, descriptive
  • Story — narrative with a plot
  • Dialogue — conversation between two people
  • Speech — address to an audience
  • Report — factual account with recommendations
  • Book/Film Review — evaluation and recommendation

General Structure for All Tasks

Introduction

  • Hook the reader with a question, quote, or interesting fact
  • Provide context / background information
  • State your thesis or main idea clearly

Body (2-3 paragraphs)

  • Each paragraph has ONE main idea
  • Start with a topic sentence
  • Support with examples, evidence, or explanations
  • Use linking words between paragraphs

Conclusion

  • Summarize your main points
  • Restate your thesis in different words
  • End with a final thought, recommendation, or call to action

BAC Success Criteria

CriterionWhat to Do
FormatCorrect structure for the task type (date, salutation for letters)
GrammarAccurate tenses, subject-verb agreement, correct articles
VocabularyRich and varied words; avoid repetition
CohesionUse linking words; logical flow of ideas
Task AchievementAddress all parts of the prompt
Length150-250 words for letters, 200-300 for essays

Common BAC Topics

Education, immigration, environment, technology, health, youth issues, women's rights, globalization, culture, human rights, citizenship, development, media, science

Sample BAC Essay Outline

Topic: Should students wear school uniforms?
Introduction: Hook (question) + context + thesis (both sides exist)
Body 1: Arguments FOR uniforms (equality, discipline, identity)
Body 2: Arguments AGAINST uniforms (creativity, comfort, cost)
Conclusion: Summary + balanced opinion + recommendation

Final Tips

  • Read the prompt carefully — underline key words.
  • Plan for 5 minutes before writing.
  • Leave 5 minutes to review your work.
  • Write neatly and clearly.
  • Stay on topic — do not include irrelevant information.
