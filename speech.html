Speech Writing (كتابة الخطاب)

Speeches are formal addresses to an audience. They require persuasive language and rhetorical devices.

Structure of a Speech

Salutation

Greet the audience appropriately.

Good morning, respected teachers and fellow students...
Mr. President, distinguished guests, ladies and gentlemen...

Introduction

Introduce yourself and state the purpose of your speech. Hook the audience.

Body (2-3 main points)

Present your arguments with evidence, stories, or statistics.

Conclusion

Summarize key points and end with a powerful closing statement or call to action.

Closing

Thank the audience.

Thank you for your attention. / Thank you for listening.

Rhetorical Devices for Speeches

DeviceExplanationExample
Rhetorical questionQuestion with no answer expectedDon't we all deserve a better future?
RepetitionRepeating words for emphasisWe must act. We must act now. We must act together.
Triplets (Rule of 3)Three parallel itemsEducation, equality, and opportunity for all.
Direct addressSpeaking directly to the audienceYou have the power to make a difference.

Sample Speech Opening

Good morning, fellow students and respected teachers.

Today, I stand before you to talk about a topic that affects each and every one of us: climate change. Have you ever wondered what kind of world we will leave for our children? The time to act is now, and each of us has a role to play.

BAC Tips

  • Start with a proper salutation based on the audience.
  • Use rhetorical questions to engage listeners.
  • Use repetition and the rule of three for impact.
  • Write as if you are speaking — use short sentences.
  • End with a memorable closing line or call to action.
  • Include the speaker's name at the end.
