Article Writing (كتابة المقال الصحفي)

Articles for school magazines or newspapers require an engaging style and clear structure.

Structure

Title

Make it catchy and relevant. Use a question, a surprising fact, or a bold statement.

Introduction

Hook the reader with a question, statistic, or anecdote. State what the article is about.

Body (2-3 paragraphs)

Present your main points with evidence, examples, and quotes. Use subheadings if appropriate.

Conclusion

Summarize your message and end with a thought-provoking statement or call to action.

Key Features of a Good Article

  • Catchy title that grabs attention
  • Engaging opening paragraph
  • Clear and logical organization
  • Use of rhetorical questions
  • Appeal to the reader's emotions and interests
  • Strong conclusion that leaves an impression

Sample BAC Article

Is Technology Making Us Lazy?

Have you ever spent the whole day scrolling through social media without doing anything productive? If your answer is yes, you are not alone. Technology has become an essential part of our daily lives, but is it making us lazy?

On the one hand, technology saves us time and effort. We can order food, shop online, and communicate with people across the world in seconds. However, this convenience has a dark side. Many young people spend hours watching videos and playing video games instead of studying or exercising.

Moreover, technology affects our concentration. With constant notifications and endless content, it has become difficult to focus on one task for a long time. This is particularly problematic for students who need to prepare for important exams like the BAC.

In my opinion, technology is a tool, and like any tool, it depends on how we use it. We should learn to manage our screen time and use technology productively. Remember, the device is not the problem — the way we use it is.

BAC Tips

  • Give your article a title.
  • Include the writer's name at the end.
  • Write in an engaging, persuasive style.
  • Use rhetorical questions to involve the reader.
  • Keep paragraphs short (3-5 sentences).
