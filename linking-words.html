Linking Words (كلمات الربط)

Linking words (also called discourse markers or connectors) connect ideas and improve the flow of your writing. Using them correctly is essential for BAC essays.

By Function

Addition (الإضافة)

and, also, moreover, furthermore, in addition, besides, as well as, not only...but also, what is more

Moreover, education is the key to development.
Not only does she study hard, but she also helps others.

Contrast (التضاد)

but, however, although, even though, whereas, while, on the other hand, nevertheless, yet, in contrast, despite, in spite of

Although the exam was difficult, I managed to pass.
Some people prefer city life, whereas others enjoy the countryside.

Note: "Although" + clause, "despite" + noun/gerund.

Cause & Effect (السبب والنتيجة)

because, since, as, because of, due to, therefore, consequently, as a result, thus, so, for this reason, hence

Due to the heavy rain, the match was cancelled.
He studied hard; consequently, he passed the exam.

Sequence (التسلسل)

first, firstly, first of all, second, third, next, then, after that, finally, eventually, lastly, subsequently, meanwhile

First of all, let's consider the causes. Secondly, we will analyze the effects.

Illustration (التوضيح)

for example, for instance, such as, like, in other words, that is to say, namely, to illustrate

Several countries, such as Morocco and Tunisia, have made progress in education.

Conclusion (الخاتمة)

in conclusion, to sum up, in summary, overall, all in all, in a nutshell, to conclude, ultimately

In conclusion, education is the most powerful weapon against poverty.

BAC Essay Writing Tips

  • Use at least 5-6 different linking words in your essay.
  • Don't start every sentence with "and" or "but" — use "moreover" and "however" instead.
  • "Moreover" and "furthermore" are formal — ideal for BAC writing.
  • Use "on the one hand / on the other hand" for balanced arguments.
  • Put a comma after most linking words at the start of a sentence.

Example BAC Paragraph

"Education plays a crucial role in personal development. Moreover, it contributes to economic growth. However, many children still lack access to quality education. This is due to poverty and inadequate infrastructure. Therefore, governments must invest more in educational systems. In conclusion, education remains the foundation of a prosperous society."
