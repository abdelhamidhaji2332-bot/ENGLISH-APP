Formal Letter Writing (الرسالة الرسمية)

Formal letters appear frequently in BAC exams. They require a specific format and formal language.

Format

Your Address
City, Postal Code
Date

Recipient's Name (if known)
Recipient's Position
Company/Organization
Address

Dear Sir / Madam / Mr. Smith / Ms. Johnson,

Subject: [Topic]

Body of the letter...

Yours faithfully (if Dear Sir/Madam) / Yours sincerely (if Dear Mr./Ms.),

[Your Full Name]

Key Phrases

PurposePhrases
OpeningI am writing to... / I would like to... / I wish to express...
RequestingI would be grateful if... / Could you please... / I would appreciate...
ComplainingI am writing to complain about... / I am dissatisfied with...
ApplyingI am writing to apply for... / I am interested in the position of...
ClosingI look forward to hearing from you. / Thank you for your consideration.

Sample BAC Formal Letter

15 Avenue Mohammed V
Casablanca, 20000
March 15, 2024

The Principal
ABC High School
Rabat

Dear Sir,

Subject: Request for Information about the Student Exchange Program

I am writing to request information about the student exchange program offered by your school.

I am a second-year BAC student at XYZ School and I am very interested in participating in this program. I would be grateful if you could send me details about the application process, requirements, and deadlines.

I would also appreciate it if you could inform me about the costs involved and the accommodation options available.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.

Yours faithfully,
Ahmed El Amrani

Tips for BAC

  • Always include your address and the date in the top right.
  • Use "Yours faithfully" when starting with "Dear Sir/Madam".
  • Use "Yours sincerely" when starting with "Dear Mr./Ms. [Name]".
  • Keep paragraphs short and focused.
  • Use formal vocabulary and no contractions.
