Expressing Purpose (التعبير عن الهدف)

There are several ways to express purpose in English. Varying them in your BAC writing shows language proficiency.

1. "to" + infinitive (لـ / من أجل)

The most common way to express purpose.

I study hard to pass the BAC exam.
She went to the store to buy some milk.

2. "in order to" / "so as to" (من أجل)

More formal than "to". "In order to" is very common in BAC essays.

She exercises daily in order to stay healthy.
He left early so as to avoid traffic.
The government raised taxes in order to fund education.

3. "for" + noun / gerund (لـ)

Use "for" with a noun or -ing form.

This tool is used for cutting paper.
I went to the store for some milk.
Thank you for helping me.

4. "so that" + clause (لكي / حتى)

Use "so that" when the subject of the purpose clause is different from the main clause. Often followed by can/could/will/would.

I'll give you my number so that you can call me.
She spoke slowly so that everyone could understand.
He saved money so that he could buy a car.

5. "to avoid" + gerund (لتجنب)

Express negative purpose.

He left early to avoid getting stuck in traffic.
She studies hard to avoid failing the exam.

6. "for fear of" / "in case" (خوفا من)

Express purpose with potential negative outcome.

She whispered for fear of waking the baby.
Take an umbrella in case it rains.

BAC Exam Tip

In your writing tasks, vary your purpose expressions. Don't just use "to" every time — use "in order to", "so that", and "for" to show range.
