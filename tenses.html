English Tenses (الأزمنة في اللغة الإنجليزية)

Understanding tenses is crucial for BAC exams. Here's a complete guide with all the key tenses you need to master.

1. Present Simple (المضارع البسيط)

Form: Subject + V1 (add s/es for he/she/it)

Use: Habits, general truths, facts, routines, schedules

She goes to school every day.
Water boils at 100°C.
The train leaves at 6 PM.

Signal words: always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, every day/week, on Mondays

2. Present Continuous (المضارع المستمر)

Form: Subject + am/is/are + V-ing

Use: Actions happening now, temporary situations, future arrangements

They are studying for the BAC exam right now.
He is working on a project this week.
I am meeting my friend tomorrow.

Signal words: now, at the moment, currently, today, this week, look!, listen!

3. Past Simple (الماضي البسيط)

Form: Subject + V2 (regular -ed / irregular form)

Use: Completed actions in the past, past states, past habits

She visited Marrakech last year.
They went to the cinema yesterday.
I lived in Casablanca when I was young.

Signal words: yesterday, last week/month/year, ago, in 2020, when I was...

4. Past Continuous (الماضي المستمر)

Form: Subject + was/were + V-ing

Use: Interrupted actions, background description, parallel actions

I was reading when the phone rang.
They were playing football at 5 PM yesterday.
While I was walking home, it started to rain.

Signal words: while, when, as, at 5 PM yesterday

5. Present Perfect (المضارع التام)

Form: Subject + have/has + V3 (past participle)

Use: Past actions with present relevance, life experiences, recent changes, unfinished actions

She has visited Paris twice.
I have finished my homework.
They have lived here for five years.

Signal words: already, just, yet, ever, never, since, for, so far, recently

6. Past Perfect (الماضي التام)

Form: Subject + had + V3

Use: Action completed before another past action (the "past of the past")

When I arrived, they had already left.
She had studied hard before she took the exam.

Signal words: after, before, by the time, already, just, never

7. Future Simple (المستقبل البسيط)

Form: Subject + will + V1

Use: Predictions, promises, spontaneous decisions, offers

It will rain tomorrow.
I will help you with your homework.
Don't worry, I will call you.

8. Future Perfect (المستقبل التام)

Form: Subject + will have + V3

Use: Action completed before a specific future time

By next year, I will have graduated.
She will have finished her project by Monday.

BAC Exam Tips (نصائح للباك)

  • Always check the signal word to determine which tense to use.
  • In writing tasks, vary your tenses to show range.
  • Be careful with irregular verbs in Past Simple and Present Perfect.
  • Practice tense transformation exercises — they appear in every BAC exam.
