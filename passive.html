Passive Voice (المبني للمجهول)

The passive voice is very common in BAC exam writing tasks, especially in formal writing like reports and articles.

Formation

Active: Subject + Verb + Object

Passive: Object + be (conjugated) + V3 + (by agent)

Active: The students wrote the essays.
Passive: The essays were written by the students.

Tense Changes

When to Use Passive Voice

  • When the agent (doer) is unknown: "My phone was stolen." (we don't know who)
  • When the agent is obvious: "The criminal was arrested." (by the police)
  • When the action is more important than the doer: "A new hospital will be built."
  • In formal/academic writing (BAC essays, reports, articles)

BAC Examples

Active: The government will build a new school.
Passive: A new school will be built by the government.
Active: Scientists have discovered a new vaccine.
Passive: A new vaccine has been discovered.

⚠️ Common BAC Mistakes

  • Forgetting to conjugate "be": "The letter was sent" (not "the letter sent")
  • Using wrong past participle for irregular verbs
  • Adding "by" when the agent is unnecessary
  • Using passive when active is more natural

BAC Writing Tip

In your BAC essay, alternate between active and passive voice for better style. Use passive in objective descriptions and active for strong statements.
