Informal Letter Writing (الرسالة غير الرسمية)

Informal letters are written to friends and family. The tone is friendly and casual, but you still need good structure.

Format

Your Address (top right)
Date (below address)

Dear [Friend's Name],

Opening: How are you? I hope you're doing well.

Body: Share news, experiences, thoughts...

Closing: Write back soon! Take care!

Best wishes / Love,
[Your Name]

Key Phrases

PurposePhrases
OpeningHow are you? / I hope this letter finds you well. / It was great to hear from you.
NewsGuess what! / You won't believe what happened! / I'm writing to tell you about...
ApologizingI'm sorry I haven't written earlier. / I apologize for not replying sooner.
ClosingI can't wait to see you. / Write back soon. / Take care of yourself.

Sample BAC Informal Letter

12 Rue de la Liberte
Fes
March 10, 2024

Dear Youssef,

How are you? I hope this letter finds you well. I'm sorry I haven't written for a while — I've been busy studying for the BAC exams.

I'm writing to share some exciting news. I've been selected for the student exchange program in Canada! I can't believe it. I'll be spending three months in Toronto next year. I'm so excited but also a little nervous.

How is your preparation for BAC going? Are you planning to apply for university this year? Let me know what's new with you.

Well, that's all for now. I hope we can meet soon and catch up properly. Write back when you have time.

Take care,
Khalid

Tips for BAC

  • Use contractions (I'm, don't, can't) — they are appropriate here.
  • Use informal vocabulary and expressions.
  • Show emotion and personality in your writing.
  • Still organize your letter into clear paragraphs.
  • Use exclamation marks for enthusiasm!
