Modal Verbs (الأفعال الناقصة)

Modal verbs are auxiliary verbs that express ability, permission, obligation, possibility, advice, and more.

Characteristics of Modals

  • They never change form (no -s in third person).
  • They are followed by base verb (V1) without "to".
  • They form negatives by adding "not".
  • They form questions by inversion.

Complete List of Modal Verbs

ModalUseExample
canAbility, permission, possibilityI can swim. / Can I go out?
couldPast ability, polite requestI could run fast when I was young. / Could you help me?
mayPermission (formal), possibilityMay I come in? / It may rain later.
mightPossibility (less certain than may)She might be late. / It might snow.
mustObligation (strong), logical certaintyYou must wear a seatbelt. / He must be home now.
have toExternal obligation (must ≠ have to)I have to wake up early for school.
shouldAdvice, recommendation, expectationYou should study more. / The train should arrive soon.
ought toAdvice (formal, less common)You ought to see a doctor.
willFuture, promise, willingnessI will call you. / I will help you.
wouldPolite request, past habitWould you open the door? / When I was young, I would play football.
shallOffer, suggestion (formal, with I/we)Shall I open the window? / Shall we go?
need toNecessity (semi-modal)You need to finish your homework.

Negative Forms

can + not = cannot / can't
must + not = mustn't (prohibition)
should + not = shouldn't
don't / doesn't have to (no obligation)
need + not = needn't

Past Modals (Modals + have + V3)

These are very common in BAC exams for expressing past situations:

FormMeaningExampleDarija
must have + V3Logical certainty about the pastHe must have forgotten the meeting.حتما نسي
might/may have + V3Possibility in the pastShe might have left early.ربما غادرت
could have + V3Ability that wasn't usedI could have passed if I had studied.كان يمكنني أن أنجح
should have + V3Past advice (unfollowed)You should have told me.كان يجب أن تخبرني
would have + V3Imaginary past resultI would have come if I had known.كنت سأتي

BAC Exam Tips

  • "Must" and "have to" are similar but not identical: must = internal obligation, have to = external.
  • "Mustn't" (prohibition) is different from "don't have to" (no obligation).
  • Use past modals (must have / should have) in third conditional essays.
