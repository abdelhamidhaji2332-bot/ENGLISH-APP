Essay Writing (كتابة المقال)

The essay is one of the most common BAC writing tasks. It assesses your ability to organize ideas and argue effectively.

Types of BAC Essays

  • Opinion Essay: Express your personal opinion on a topic
  • Argumentative Essay: Present arguments for and against
  • Descriptive Essay: Describe a place, person, or experience
  • Cause and Effect Essay: Explain causes and consequences
  • Problem-Solution Essay: Identify a problem and propose solutions

Standard Essay Structure

Introduction

  • Hook: Start with a question, quote, or interesting fact
  • Background: Provide context about the topic
  • Thesis Statement: Your main argument in one clear sentence

Body Paragraph 1

  • Topic sentence (first main point)
  • Explanation and analysis
  • Example or evidence
  • Concluding sentence (link to next paragraph)

Body Paragraph 2

  • Topic sentence (second main point)
  • Explanation and analysis
  • Example or evidence
  • Concluding sentence

Body Paragraph 3 (optional)

  • Counter-argument or third point
  • Rebuttal or further elaboration

Conclusion

  • Restate thesis (different words)
  • Summarize main points
  • Final thought / recommendation / future outlook

Useful Phrases for Essays

FunctionPhrases
Introducing opinionsIn my opinion... / I strongly believe that... / It is widely argued that...
Adding pointsMoreover... / Furthermore... / In addition... / What is more...
Showing contrastHowever... / On the other hand... / Nevertheless... / Although...
Giving examplesFor instance... / For example... / Such as... / To illustrate...
ConcludingIn conclusion... / To sum up... / Overall... / All things considered...

Sample BAC Essay Outline

Topic: Should recycling be compulsory?
Introduction: With environmental crises increasing, recycling has become more important than ever. This essay will argue that recycling should be made compulsory.
Body 1: Environmental benefits — reduces waste, saves resources
Body 2: Economic benefits — creates jobs, saves money
Body 3: Counter-argument — personal freedom vs. collective good (rebuttal)
Conclusion: Recycling is essential for our planet's future; governments must act now.

BAC Essay Tips

  • Always plan before writing (5 minutes).
  • Write 4-5 paragraphs (introduction + 2-3 body + conclusion).
  • Use at least 5 different linking words.
  • Stay on topic — every sentence should support your thesis.
  • Proofread for grammar and spelling mistakes.
