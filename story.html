Story Writing (كتابة القصة)

Story writing tests your creativity and ability to use narrative tenses effectively.

Elements of a Good Story

  • Characters: Who is in the story? Give them names and personalities.
  • Setting: Where and when does the story take place?
  • Plot: What happens? (beginning, middle, end)
  • Conflict: What problem or challenge arises?
  • Resolution: How is the problem solved?
  • Theme: What is the message or lesson?

Story Structure

Introduction (Exposition)

Introduce characters, setting, and the initial situation.

Rising Action

Describe events that build up to the main conflict.

Climax

The most exciting or important moment — the turning point.

Falling Action

Events after the climax leading to resolution.

Resolution

How the story ends — what happens to the characters?

Narrative Tenses

TenseUse in Stories
Past SimpleMain events in order
Past ContinuousBackground description, interrupted actions
Past PerfectEarlier events (flashbacks)
Would / Used toPast habits or repeated actions

Useful Story Vocabulary

suddenly, immediately, eventually, finally, once upon a time, later, meanwhile, after a while, all of a sudden, in the end, unfortunately, luckily

Sample Story Opening

It was a cold winter evening when Ahmed decided to take a shortcut through the old forest. The wind was howling, and the trees looked like dark shadows against the gray sky. Little did he know that this decision would change his life forever...

BAC Tips

  • Use a variety of narrative tenses (Past Simple, Past Continuous, Past Perfect).
  • Show emotions through actions and dialogue, not just telling.
  • Include descriptive details (sights, sounds, feelings).
  • Make sure the story has a clear beginning, middle, and end.
  • Keep it between 150-250 words for BAC.
